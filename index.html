    Methodology & sources

    GOP vs. expectations meter: a model read comparing actual margins in races with meaningful counts (plus AP/decision-desk calls) against final polling averages and 2024/2022 margins; "worse" or "better" only when Republicans are clearly behind or ahead in multiple races, otherwise "mixed / as expected." It is not a call.

      Races are marked called only when AP or a network decision desk calls them. Projections are an independent model, not calls. Prediction-market figures, if shown, are trader odds, not forecasts. Every figure links to its source. No tracking, no ads, no sign-ups. Data feed: data.json.
